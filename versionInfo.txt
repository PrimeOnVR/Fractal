# About

# Joining

You can join your friends via e4mc, accessible by simply opening a world to LAN, and then checking your chat for an IP. Others may be required to use Fractal to join your world, due to QOL tie-ins.

# Multiplayer

If you would like to experience the full Fractal experience, with all mods and features, you must put "-Dfabric.addMods=.\fractal\extraMods\hasMultiplayerRisk" in your java arguments. By adding this java argument, you acknowledge the risks of using potentially non multiplayer-friendly mods!

Multiplayer mod risks depend from server to server, certain mods such as Controllify can get you flagged on servers due to its ultra-fluid movement!

**MOST** mods relating to weird movement and obvious cheats have been disabled and put in hasMultiplayerRisk, however, certain non-hack mods may be considered a competitive advantage based on the server, like Xaero's Minimap and World Map! Check your servers rules and the mods you have enabled!
